Bhava: The Twelve Houses

Privacy Policy · Starward · Last updated 27 October 2026

Bhava is a card game built on your Vedic birth chart. It was made to need as little from you as possible. This page explains exactly what it keeps and where.

Your birth details stay on your phone

The date, time and place of birth you enter, and the birth details of anyone you add for compatibility, are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. The chart is calculated on the phone, without an internet connection.

No account, no sign-in

Bhava has no accounts and no login. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location or any other personal information.

Your progress stays on your phone

Your runs, karma, readings, journal, settings and game progress are saved on your device only. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, they are deleted. We cannot recover them, because we never had them.

Purchases

The one-time "Full reading" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy; we never see your payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether this Google account owns the purchase, so that it can be restored after a reinstall.

Reminders

If you turn on the morning reminder, the phone schedules it itself. Nothing is sent from a server, and you can turn it off at any time in the app or in your phone's settings.

No tracking and no ads in this version

This version of Bhava contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. If a future version adds optional rewarded ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and it will say what the ad provider collects.

Children

Bhava is meant for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Readings

Readings are for entertainment and self-reflection. They are not advice of any kind.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email address shown on Bhava's Google Play page.
